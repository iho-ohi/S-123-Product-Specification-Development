<title id="_0427c304-cf37-9e07-b49c-c924814e0f07">Feature</title>
<p id="_06d11aaf-acbf-21a6-421f-f2e550aaf2e0">A feature contains descriptive attributes that characterize real world entities.</p>

<p id="_9aeece56-0f5a-8847-36f7-01339fdc2d43">The word ‘feature’ as used in the ISO 191xx series and in S-100 based product specifications has two distinct but related senses — ’feature type’ and ‘feature instance’. A feature instance is a single occurrence of the feature and is represented as an object in a dataset.</p>
